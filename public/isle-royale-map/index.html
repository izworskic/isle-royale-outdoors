What this map is built to answer

Isle Royale is one island, but not one kind of trip.

First visit

Orient Rock Harbor and Windigo, then see how trails, camps, docks and visitor facilities relate to each other before you pick a route.

Backpacking

Start with the trail/camp network. Use the current NPS mileage table and camping rules for the distances and restrictions that actually govern the trip.

Boating & paddling

Every designated NPS portage is on the map with its official distance, terrain and both landings, so you can see how the interior lakes connect before committing a day to a carry. Switch on the nautical chart for Lake Superior depths, buoys and hazards.

Driving the big lake instead? Open the Lake Superior Circle Tour planner.

Deep exploration

Start with the lightweight six-class vegetation overview or USGS shaded relief, then step into detailed vegetation, geologic units, 1996–2017 vegetation change and the 2021 Horne Fire with every source date visible.

Sources & methodology

This guide keeps current National Park Service (NPS) authority, public map geometry, and research/reference data separate. The full source strategy and source desk live on their own page.
