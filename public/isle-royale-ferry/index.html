Why two ferry schedules matter

Sea Hunter III serves Windigo and, on selected dates, can support a same-day visit. Voyageur II can reach Rock Harbor and scheduled intermediate island docks, but its normal circumnavigation spans two days. A one-day visit to Rock Harbor from Grand Portage is not a valid assumption. Published sailing patterns and dock stops depend on date and advance reservations.

The operator requires you to reserve the return leg when you book outbound travel. Intermediate docks such as McCargoe Cove and Belle Isle may not be served without existing reservations. The tool does not quote a price for those intermediate connections until the specific ticket pair can be validated.

Time zone trap

Grand Portage operates on Central Time. Isle Royale follows Eastern Time, one hour ahead. The ferry operator publishes its boat clock times in Central; do not shift your booking departure by an hour when crossing to the island.

Park fees and camping permits are separate from the boat ticket

NPS charges $7 per visitor age 16 and older per calendar day, including arrival and departure, unless covered by an eligible pass (one pass typically covers the holder plus three adults, not an unlimited group). Overnight camping parties of 1–6 get a backcountry permit when they arrive; groups of seven or more must arrange their group camping permit in advance. Your ferry fare does not represent the complete trip cost. Marina parking is separately charged, but no unverified parking rate is estimated here.
