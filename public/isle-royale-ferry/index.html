    03 / THE CROSSING · REAL GEOGRAPHY

    Where the ferries actually take you.

    Grand Portage is on Minnesota's north shore. Windigo sits on Isle Royale's western tip. Rock Harbor is much farther along the island's northeastern end. These are different journeys—not alternative names for the same stop.

    The Windigo day boat

    Sea Hunter III

    In the published 2026 pattern, this boat left Grand Portage and returned on the same day, giving visitors a few hours around Windigo before the afternoon departure. It is the natural boat to investigate for a first-time, no-overnight Windigo visit.

    2026 reference: 8:30 AM CT departure, 10 AM CT Windigo arrival, 2 PM CT island departure. These are historical examples—not future operating hours.

    The island-circling boat

    Voyageur II

    Voyageur II follows the northern side of Isle Royale toward Rock Harbor on the outbound day and the southern shore on its return circuit. It can serve remote docks by advance arrangement. Reaching Rock Harbor from Grand Portage and returning requires an overnight itinerary—not a same-day shortcut.

    Intermediate docks depend on actual bookings and operating arrangements; no guaranteed pickup is inferred from the schedule.

    Clock discipline

    The island is one hour ahead.

    Grand Portage departs on Central Time. Isle Royale observes Eastern Time, one hour later. A published 10 AM CT arrival corresponds to 11 AM ET on the island. Keep the operator's Central clock when reading its timetable.

    Park entrance

    The ferry is not the park fee.

    NPS lists $7 per person age 16+ per calendar day, including arrival and departure days, unless covered by an eligible pass. One qualifying pass covers the holder and up to three other adults; a large party is not automatically free.

    Sleep on the island

    Backcountry permits are separate.

    Small overnight camping parties of 1–6 receive their permits on arrival. Groups of seven or more need advance NPS arrangements. Ferry seats, overnight accommodations and permits are different commitments.

    GRAND PORTAGE FERRY QUESTIONS

    Before you book the boat.

    Answers for departures from Minnesota. All listed fares and operating weekdays refer to the completed 2026 season, not available tickets for 2027.

    How much is the ferry from Grand Portage to Isle Royale?

    In 2026, Sea Hunter III's Windigo day-trip round trip was $120.50 per adult and $110.50 per child. Voyageur II's standard Grand Portage leg was $110.50 per adult one way, or $221 for two legs. Park entrance, parking and gear charges cost extra. Verify 2027 fares with the operator.

    Which days does the Grand Portage Isle Royale ferry run?

    The 2026 Sea Hunter III ran Wednesday through Saturday, plus Sundays during its July 15 to August 16 peak period. Voyageur II used different outbound and inbound days; see both dated operating tables above. The 2027 Grand Portage schedules are not yet published.

    Can I take a one-day trip to Isle Royale from Minnesota?

    Yes. Sea Hunter III's published Windigo day trip from Grand Portage allowed four hours on the island in 2026. There is no same-day Grand Portage ferry visit to Rock Harbor; the Voyageur II route needs an overnight itinerary.

    Use the Isle Royale map for trails, docks and campgrounds.

    Where do I book Sea Hunter III and Voyageur II tickets?

    Book directly with Grand Portage Isle Royale Transportation Lines through its official reservation page, and reserve return passage with outbound passage. As of October 12, 2026, the operator has not confirmed available 2027 sailings or opened those reservations.

    Official ferry booking page ↗

    Where do I board, and what about marina parking?

    Both ferries board at Grand Portage Marina, 402 Upper Road, Grand Portage, Minnesota. The Village of Grand Portage charges a separate marina parking fee. The ferry fare does not include that fee.

    Why does the Isle Royale ferry timetable use Central Time?

    Grand Portage, Minnesota uses Central Time, and the ferry operator publishes its schedule on that clock. Isle Royale uses Eastern Time, one hour ahead. Follow the operator's published boarding and return times.

    Can I take my car to Isle Royale National Park?

    No. Isle Royale has no automobile ferry or road connection to the mainland. Leave your vehicle in the designated mainland parking area and explore on foot or with permitted watercraft.

    What happens if Lake Superior weather delays the ferry?

    Weather and transport disruptions can delay a crossing or even a return from a day trip. Consult operator policies and National Park Service advice, and bring medication, additional food and warm layers for an unexpected delay.
